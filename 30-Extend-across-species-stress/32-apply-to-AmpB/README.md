To determine the applicability of FungaLight-based survival assay beyond oxidative stress, we applied the above protocol to C. glabrata cells treated with Amphotericin B (AmpB), a key antifungal used to treat systemic candidiasis (Pappas et al., 2018). In this case we found an excellent agreement between CFU and FungaLight quantified survival rates (R2 = 0.88), likely because AmpB quickly kills the yeast cells rather than causing recoverable damages.
