Here, Hanxi has used FungaLight to quantify post-oxidative-stress survival in two additional yeast species, i.e., _S. cerevisiae_ and _C. albicans_. Because these two species have much lower level of resistance to H2O2, Hanxi modified the hydrogen peroxide concentrations from 0, 10, 100 and 1000 mM used for _C. glabrata_ to 0, 0.6, 6, and 1000 mM. The last, 1 M concentration was kept as a lethal dose.
